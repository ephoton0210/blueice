## Regex isolation

Run the standalone regress algorithm in a sibling `bluejs-regexp-worker` process. Both compilation (including parser literal validation) and matching use a bounded request/reply channel. A parent deadline covers worker IO and algorithm execution; timeout kills and reaps the worker and joins its IO thread before returning a resource error. Later operations start a fresh worker. Do not use detached matching threads or cooperative cancellation as a hard timeout. UTF-16 input and capture ranges/names cross the protocol losslessly. Cache one compiled pattern per worker; no compiled regex executes in the BlueJS process. Discover the installed sibling binary or an explicitly configured `BLUEJS_REGEXP_WORKER` path; fail closed if unavailable. The host must ship the helper beside its executable. Defaults and configurable VM limits will be tested through the public pipeline, including catastrophic backtracking and recovery.

## Internationalization

**Historical baseline notice.** The detailed paragraph below was written against ECMA-402 edition 13. The normative current-public baseline is the ECMAScript 2027 text identified in Phase 25's [conformance matrix](../phase-25-ecma402-internationalization/CONFORMANCE.md); this document's dated implementation notes do not override it.

Target published ECMA-402 edition 13 (June 2026), verified at the [official publication page](https://ecma-international.org/publications-and-standards/standards/ecma-402/), rather than the living 2027 draft. ICU supplies locale/case/collation algorithms and data; BlueJS implements observable argument conversions, option order, builtin objects/descriptors and exceptions. Scope follows the preceding String task: Intl.Collator, Intl.getCanonicalLocales, Intl.Locale, its Locale-info query methods and the three locale-sensitive String methods. `Intl.NumberFormat` now delegates `format`, `formatToParts`, `formatRange`, `formatRangeToParts`, `resolvedOptions` and `supportedLocalesOf` to the host-neutral Phase 25 service. Its typed mathematical-value bridge preserves exact base-10 strings and BigInts rather than first converting them to `f64`; the range methods also preserve ECMA-402 part ownership. The provider carries full resolved-locale decimal, compact and currency data, and the pinned NumberFormat Test262 inventory passes. `Intl.DateTimeFormat` now owns its branded service state and implements construction/call, `supportedLocalesOf`, bound `format`, `formatToParts`, `formatRange`, `formatRangeToParts` and `resolvedOptions` through ICU4X date/time semantic skeletons. Its exact requested field set is selected at runtime, including weekday/era, partial calendar fields, hour-only and fractional-second forms; unsupported ICU4X combinations are formed from a semantic superset and remove only unrequested fields while preserving field order. Its direct, versioned Jiff bundle pins IANA TZDB `2026c`, exposes that release at runtime, case-normalizes every bundled IANA Zone-or-Link identifier without silently replacing a Link with its target, accepts and normalizes ECMA-402 `±HH`, `±HHMM` and `±HH:MM` fixed offsets, and calculates offset/DST transitions independently of machine zoneinfo. A valid TimeClip endpoint outside Jiff's civil-year range uses its equivalent position in the Gregorian 400-year cycle to select a named zone's recurring IANA offset; this preserves the ISO fields and weekday rather than rejecting the endpoint, but is not historical zone data for that remote year. `formatRange` and `formatRangeToParts` reject an `undefined` endpoint before either `ToNumber`, retain ordinary `ToNumber` and `TimeClip`, and do not add a start-after-end rejection. For Gregorian ranges containing a date and requesting neither a zone name nor fractional seconds, the production default is ICU4X's CLDR `DateRangeFormatter`. ICU's side spans are normalized to ECMA-402 serialized-part ownership: an equal field emitted once is `shared`, a repeated field remains endpoint-owned, and literals adjacent to a shared field or bridging the endpoints are `shared`. The ECMA-402 whole-pattern branch remains: if the formatted endpoints are identical, every part is `shared`. The bounded compatibility formatter is intentionally separate, not an equivalent ECMA-402 range implementation: it remains in use for requested zone names, time-only and fractional-second skeletons, non-Gregorian calendars, a host's explicit ICU4X opt-out, and Temporal plain values. It preserves current output while ICU4X gains the required input/data paths. `Temporal` has a deliberately bounded typed-value bridge for all four DateTimeFormat formatting entry points: ISO Instant strings are parsed to checked epoch nanoseconds; `Instant` keeps ordinary time-zone formatting while selecting its type-specific defaults; ISO plain date/time types preserve their local calendar fields, ignore the formatter time zone and suppress its zone name; and calendars/types are checked before direct or range formatting. `Temporal.ZonedDateTime` remains rejected by the DateTimeFormat methods, while `Temporal.ZonedDateTime.prototype.toLocaleString` delegates with the value's own time zone. This is not a claim of a complete Temporal API. ICU4X 2.3.0 is consumed through the pinned compatibility fork and remains an implementation slice rather than a claim of full DateTimeFormat or ECMA-402 conformance: localized long zone names and broader Test262 validation remain required. DisplayNames, DurationFormat, ListFormat, PluralRules, RelativeTimeFormat, Segmenter and supportedValuesOf retain bounded data coverage. The complete non-Temporal pinned `intl402/` selection passes **2,656/2,656 modes** (1,328 files; 803.9 seconds; `intl402/Temporal/` explicitly excluded), validating the present API surface without claiming complete CLDR, Temporal, or ECMA-402 conformance.
